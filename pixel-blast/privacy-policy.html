Privacy Policy for Pixel Blast

App Package / Bundle ID: com.pixelblast.arcade · Developer: Pixel Blast Studio · Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Pixel Blast Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the mobile game Pixel Blast as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use Pixel Blast on Android and iOS devices.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Pixel Blast does not require user accounts, sign-in, or registration. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

Your gameplay preferences (such as control handling settings, sound settings, best scores, and in-game coins) are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising & SDK Data Collection (Google AdMob)

Pixel Blast uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain data to deliver, personalize (where permitted), measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, including:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only when granted via App Tracking Transparency on iOS).
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location inferred from your IP address.
  • App Activity & Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and basic interaction events.
  • Diagnostics & Device Information: Device model, OS version, crash logs, and SDK performance telemetry.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Your Privacy Choices & Controls

  • EEA & UK Users (GDPR / UMP): Where required by law, Pixel Blast requests your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time using the Ad Privacy button inside the app.
  • iOS Tracking Control: You can enable or disable tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android Advertising ID Control: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Pixel Blast is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention & Security

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall Pixel Blast. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's retention and security policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
