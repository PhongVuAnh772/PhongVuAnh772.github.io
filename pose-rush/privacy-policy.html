Privacy Policy for Pose Rush

App Package / Bundle ID: com.poserush.game · Developer: Pixel Blast Studio · Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Pixel Blast Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the mobile game Pose Rush. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled, used, and shared when you play Pose Rush on Android and iOS devices in either Tap Mode or Camera CV Mode.

1. Camera Access & On-Device Body Pose Detection

100% On-Device Processing: Pose Rush never records, saves, transmits, or uploads photos, video frames, or body-landmark data from your camera.
  • Camera permission is strictly optional. You can play all campaign levels and Endless Mode in Tap Mode without ever granting camera access.
  • When you choose Camera CV Mode, live front-camera frames are processed locally in temporary device memory using Apple Vision (iOS) or Google ML Kit Pose Detection (Android) solely to match your real-time body pose against incoming game walls.
  • Every camera frame and pose landmark is discarded immediately after processing. No visual or biometric data ever leaves your device.

2. Personal Information & Local Game Data

Pose Rush does not require user accounts, sign-in, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. Your gameplay progress (unlocked levels, Endless best score, unlocked skins and walls, audio settings, and Remove Ads entitlement) is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

To keep Pose Rush free to play, the app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission).
  • Approximate IP-derived location, device model, operating system version, app interactions, and ad performance diagnostics (used for frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention).
  • EEA, UK & Switzerland Users (GDPR / UMP): Where required by law, Pose Rush requests your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before serving personalized ads.
  • Managing Ad Preferences: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. On iOS 14.5+, you can manage tracking anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. In-App Purchases

Pose Rush offers an optional one-time non-consumable purchase to Remove Ads (com.poserush.game.remove_ads). All billing, payment processing, and receipt verification are handled exclusively by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your credit card or billing information. You can restore your purchase at any time using the Restore Purchases option in Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Pose Rush is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will promptly assist.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Pose Rush stores gameplay data locally on your device and does not maintain remote user accounts, you can delete all local app data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling Pose Rush. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's retention and privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
